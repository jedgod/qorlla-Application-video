Help & Tutorials

Short guides to get started.

  • Joining a meeting - use Join Meeting, enter the ID or link, check your camera and microphone, then join.
  • Chat - create a conversation, add people, share files.
  • Phone - place and receive calls once your workspace has telephony enabled.
  • Docs - create documents and share them with specific people.
  • Clips - record short videos to share.
  • Privacy - your account keeps your mail, notes and calendar private.
