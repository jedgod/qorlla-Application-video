User Guide

  • Joining a meeting - use Join Meeting, enter the ID or link, check your camera and microphone, then join.
  • Chat - create a conversation, add people, share files.
  • Phone - place and receive calls once your workspace has telephony enabled.
  • Docs - create documents and share them with specific people.
  • Clips - record short videos to share.
  • Privacy - your account keeps your mail, notes and calendar private.

FAQ

Do I need an account to join a meeting?
No. Guests can join a meeting they were invited to with its ID or invitation link.
Why can't I use Chat, Phone, Docs or Clips as a guest?
Those are private to signed-in members of a workspace.
My camera or microphone does not work.
Allow access in your browser's site settings, then use Test microphone and camera on the pre-join screen. You can still join without them.
