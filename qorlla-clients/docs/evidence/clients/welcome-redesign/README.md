## What changed
- Welcome screen: compact one-page layout scaled from window width and height, 48 dp touch targets, meeting-ID input fills its box.
- Web phone tab bar (<= 700 px): Home, Chat, Phone, Docs, Clips, More share one row without scrolling; the other pages are tiles on More. Pages opened from the tab bar have a close button (X, Escape) that returns to the page before.
- One tab per account per browser (`assets/single-tab.js`, workspace and chat share a lock). A convenience only, not a security boundary; where Web Locks do not exist nothing is enforced and `data-single-tab="unsupported"` says so.
- Invitation page: way-in options chosen for the device (app where one exists, otherwise an account); download buttons only for https URLs set in `QORLLA_DOWNLOAD_*_URL`, none by default. The guest navigation is hidden on the invitation page until the person signs in.
- Old built-in room (`/index.html`): no longer used for an invited guest or inside the mobile app. A guest goes to the Qorlla Meet video page or is told video is unavailable. It stays for web hosts. The landing on the real Meet page was NOT exercised end to end here (no Meet); it is covered by a stubbed redirect test only.
- Desktop shell: `npm run dev:local` opens one existing server; the shell has no database or server of its own.

Screenshots show only these screens (no camera preview).

Known gaps: native text is translated for es/fr/pt-PT but the chosen language is not stored across restarts; the hero artwork is a vector redraw; the Portuguese numeric-field placeholder is cut off at 320 dp; TalkBack was not run; the LG phone was never launched; downloads, installers and PSTN stay disabled and unpublished.

### Meet AI consent and verifiable citations (branch feature/qorlla-ai-review-panel)
- **Consent layers** (all must hold): host attestation in Workplace (`meeting_ai_consents`, unchanged) AND, per author, Meet's own append-only `ai_consents` (separate purposes `transcription` and `summary`) AND the host's open AI request in Meet AND the AI Gateway's workspace authorisation / approved provider. Recording consent (`recording_consents.ai` is always false) is a different record and never answers AI.
- **Meet** (migration `v4_19_ai_request.sql`, 2 columns on `meetings`): host request/close, prompt for everyone present and for late joiners, per-purpose agree/withdraw in Settings, read-only export for the portal (`/api/integrations/meetings/:id/ai-consent`, chat export now carries participant ids). Same hash-chained audit as recording.
- **Portal** (migration `009_meet_ai_consent.sql`): only consenting authors' messages are ever stored or sent; re-checked before the model call and again immediately before publishing; revocation discards transcript and result; consent audit trail (counts and codes only, append-only); `meet_ai` kill switch; report citations column.
- **Citations**: signed references (ids, version, position, text hash; no text). The backend writes every identifier: model-suggested quotes / cell ranges / slide numbers are kept only if they resolve to real content; Meet decisions and action items are grounded by the backend in the consented transcript; QorllaMate sources are what retrieval actually returned. Opening re-checks access, consent, retention and the hash (403 / 404 / 409 / 410 with a stated reason).
- **Defect found and fixed**: a latent deadlock between a host's revocation and a running AI job (worker held the job row and needed a workspace share lock; revocation held the workspace and needed the job). Lock order is now workspace, job, transcript everywhere; 5 runs x 20 interleavings, no deadlock logged afterwards.
- **Results**: portal 246/246; gateway unit 21, gateway routes 26, refusal 6, Meet consent 23, meeting AI 27, migrations 10. Browser: Meet AI consent 3/3 (real Meet + real DB, including the portal's own client reading Meet's real export), panel 9/9, Docs/Sheets/Slides/QorllaMate on `qai` 18 passed + 1 skipped by design, production-default `qnoai` 9 passed + 10 skipped (AI off). Meet regression on `qai`: 22 passed, 3 failed: screen-share and two-browser route tests fail identically on the unmodified Meet build (environment); one layout-while-sharing test failed 2 of 11 runs on the new build and passed 14 of 14 afterwards and 8 of 8 on baseline (cause not established, UNVERIFIED).
- **Limitations**: a withdrawal landing between the final Meet check and the database commit (milliseconds, two systems) is not atomic; consent withdrawn AFTER a report is published does not rewrite that report (the host can revoke the meeting's consent, which withdraws it); an imported transcript cannot be split by person, so it requires an open request and no objector; display names in Meet are typed by participants and unverified; a participant who answers in Meet is not authenticated beyond their Meet session.

### Consent fence, scheduled-meeting end-to-end and Meet regression comparison (branch feature/qorlla-ai-review-panel)
- **Consent fence** (`qorlla-portal/docs/CONSENT_FENCE.md`): Meet numbers every AI-consent decision (`v4_20`); a report records the numbers of the agreements it rests on (`consent_basis`, portal migration `010`); every read asks Meet again and shows the report only while nobody included has objected after those numbers; if Meet cannot be asked nothing is shown. A violation withdraws the report irreversibly and appends an audit row (no existing audit row is touched). 50 racing readers, revocation in the commit gap (with and without an outage), a failed COMMIT, worker retries, cancellation of a running job, imports and same-name participants are tested.
- **Scheduled-meeting end-to-end** (`e2e/scheduled-meeting.spec.js`, 7/7 on the AI test project): real scheduling route, PostgreSQL sync worker, Meet (real server, DB and socket), portal UI, the portal's real AI worker, both databases; a SIGKILL of the portal in the middle of a model call, recovered by the restarted worker with attempts 1 -> 2 and exactly one report, one usage record and one publication event. Evidence (ids and counts only): `ai/scheduled-meeting-evidence.json`.
- **Harness stand-in, stated plainly:** the main app's direct hand-off to Meet is switched off in this topology, so the host credential is read back from the sync worker's vault and used with the same URL-fragment hand-off the real launch uses; the four participants are real Meet sessions over Meet's API and socket (the prompts themselves are verified in a real browser by `e2e/meet-ai-consent.spec.js`).
- **Test host condition found:** OneDrive (~30,000 CPU-seconds), Defender and the WSL VM keep this Windows host at 100% CPU; with six browsers in a mesh, Meet stalled for 30 s at a time. The scenario therefore uses two browsers and generous waits (assertions unchanged). This also explains the layout-during-sharing result below.
- **Meet regression comparison** (`meet-regression/`, controlled A/B, alternating order, same host, host load recorded per run, traces kept outside git): layout-while-sharing failed 9/12 on the new build and 10/12 on the unmodified baseline (commit 5e74232) with the identical assertion (a tile 497 px wide, expected > 994).
